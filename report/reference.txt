Primary Source (Most Direct Support)
Laasmaa et al. (2011) - "Application of regularized Richardson–Lucy algorithm for deconvolution of confocal microscopy images"

Journal: Journal of Microscopy, Vol. 243(2), pp. 124-140

Key quote: "The estimated total variation regularization parameter can be monitored during deconvolution process and used as a stopping criterion"

Finding: "An inverse relation between the optimal regularization parameter and the peak signal-to-noise ratio of an image is shown"

Optimal iteration: Found to be 8th step when MSE = 0.00635

SNR-dependent: Optimal iterations vary between 8-25 depending on SNR

Why This is Standard Practice:
Paper Support: PSF Impact on Deconvolution
From the "On-the-fly estimation of a microscopy PSF" paper:

"A proper estimation of realistic point-spread function (PSF) in optical microscopy can significantly improve the deconvolution performance"

Translation: To evaluate PSF quality, you measure how well deconvolution works with that PSF

Paper Support: PSF Validation Method
From the PSF validation paper:

"We compute what those images ought to look like if our chosen PSF model were correct. We then use a confidence interval approach to see if the actual pixels match what the math predicts"

Translation: They validate PSF by comparing deconvolved output with actual data

Paper Support: PSF Evaluation Metric
From the PSF estimation benchmark paper:

"When the recovered PSFs are used with spatially-variant and regularized Richardson-Lucy deconvolution, we observed up to 2.1 dB better Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) compared to other Blind Deconvolution techniques"

Translation: They evaluate PSF by measuring SNR improvement after deconvolution
